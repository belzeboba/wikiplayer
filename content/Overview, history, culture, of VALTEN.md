### Etymology
**"Valten"** derives from the angel Valt'ael, the patron angel of the family that created Lathorn. The god that made Valt'ael, the second angel ever created, is no longer around.

#### Religion
**Usually, people worship whoever they wish.** There are however, places where some are more common. colored text.

Lathorn has a strong religious identity, with it's main worship being the goddess of light, Selos, as well as Ga'alliel, Garros and Bastion, whose churches aid in the ruling of Lathorn as The Holy Council.

Necrin doesn't involve churches into the structure in the slightest. Still, it has its popular gods. Those would be Murgaal, Garros, Yisserra, Man in Black and Kamael. Yisserra is the only god without temples in the area, Necrin being the only place that respected her wish of not having any.

In Cache, only three are the most prominent in belief. Eldask, Raijin and Fuujin. The latter two are usually most known in the continent of Seiloon.

Haelen houses the black pool within it, which is believed to be the casting of the first curse, and the place where Hexe was born. Baba yaga is rumored to be the first hag to discover, and then worship her.

### Culture

#### Calendar
**The year** is divided into six cycles. colored text.

mask - ruin - tree - star - blood [link.](https://toyhou.se/~world/233644.wiki-wip/page/173649.wiki-old-version#) Each cycle has roughly between 59-62 days.

At the end of the second cycle, the moon begins to shine through for about 6 days. It is known as "The Lunacy", the time when Eon's power is at its strongest. During that time, people avoid going out at night entirely, some board the doors and windows, some hide completely, and some might even kill their farm animals if there's no place to hide them.

Going out in the moonlight may cause any type of madness on an individual, whereas the animals and monsters can become even more dangerous or gain unstable sentience. The act itself is also seen as worship to Eon.
